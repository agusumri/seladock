A dock that replaces your taskbar, quietly.

SelaDock sits at the bottom of your screen as a dark glass bar: your pinned apps, the windows you have open, a tray flyout, and the time. It hides the Windows taskbar while it runs, and gives it back when you quit.

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) · No .NET install needed · A separate app from the Sela notch

What it does

Small bar, your whole desktop.

Everything the taskbar gave you, in a dock that reads as part of the desktop.

  • Your pinned apps

    On first run it imports the apps you already pinned to the taskbar. Drag an app onto the dock to pin it, drag an icon to reorder, right-click to unpin.

  • Running windows

    Open windows show as icons, grouped per app, with a dot underneath and a highlight for the active one. Click to focus or minimize.

  • Tray flyout

    The “^” button opens a dark popup of the apps running in the background, each one a click away from the foreground.

  • System at a glance

    Network, volume, and battery each have a button that opens the genuine Windows panel. The clock opens the calendar.

  • Replaces the taskbar

    Hides the Windows taskbar while it runs so nothing overlaps, and restores it when you quit. Reserve space or auto-hide, your choice.

  • Your size

    Auto fits the dock to your screen, or pick Small, Medium, or Large from the tray menu. Dark menus throughout, to match.

Light on your PC

Built to stay out of the way.

SelaDock does no work while the desktop is idle. It listens for window events instead of polling, extracts each icon once, and wakes the clock about once a minute.

  • Event-driven window tracking, not a busy loop
  • Idle CPU effectively zero
  • A single self-contained .exe, no install of .NET
  • Runs for your account only, no admin rights
  • ~0%idle CPU
  • .NET 10WPF, native Win32
  • MITopen source
